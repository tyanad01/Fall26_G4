## 1. Meeting Purpose
The purpose of this meeting was to review the School System Website project, discuss the prototype screens, identify the types of users, review the product backlog, and plan the tasks for Sprint 2.

## 2. Product Overview
The School System Website is designed to organize school information and make it easier to manage students, courses, faculty, and buildings. The website will include a login page, a dashboard, and database-management screens.

The website will manage six main tables: Student, Course, Faculty, Student-Course, Faculty-Course, and Building.

## 3. Types of Users
The intended users of the website are:

- **Administrators:** Manage student, course, faculty, and building records.
- **Faculty Members:** View relevant course and faculty information.
- **Students:** Access relevant student and course information.

## 4. Prototype Screens Discussed
The following prototype screens were identified for the project:

1. **Login Screen:** Allows users to access the system.
2. **Dashboard:** Provides navigation to the main sections of the website.
3. **Student Management Screen:** Displays student information, including student ID, first name, last name, address, email, phone number, and major.
4. **Course Management Screen:** Organizes course information.
5. **Faculty Management Screen:** Organizes faculty information.
6. **Student-Course Screen:** Represents the relationship between students and courses.
7. **Faculty-Course Screen:** Represents the relationship between faculty members and courses.
8. **Building Management Screen:** Organizes building information.

## 5. Design Ideas
The proposed design will use a clean, organized layout with a soft pink and white color scheme. The navigation bar will use pink (#ff99cc), and the background will use a light pink color (#fef6fb). The Poppins font will be used for a consistent appearance. Buttons, forms, and navigation links should be easy to understand and use.
